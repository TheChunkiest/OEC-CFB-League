# OEC CFB League Hub

Official Mid-American Conference (MAC) Dynasty Hub for the **Original Entertaining Crackers (OEC)** College Football 27 League.

## Features Included
- **Coach & Commissioner Login Isolation**: Every coach has a login account (`1,000 Credits/Season` sportsbook bankroll, prior-week Game of the Week voting, and End-of-Season Awards + "Holy Sh*t" Moments voting). Commissioner tools are strictly isolated to **Chunky** (who has both Commissioner and Coach capabilities).
- **Red, Black & Gold OEC Broadcast Theme**: Official OEC League Logo, Current Season & Current Week graphic header, and official Team Primary Colors next to every team name.
- **Vegas Sportsbook & Dynamic Odds Engine**: Spreads, Moneylines (`+/-`), and Over/Unders dynamically update across all subsequent weeks after every advancement, paired with an interactive **Week-to-Week Championship Odds Line Chart** and **Preseason Baseline Odds**.
- **Automated Storylines & Hot/Cold Power Rankings**: Generates 3–5 weekly storylines from recent results and computes automated Power Rankings based on Overperforming vs. Underperforming with `+/-` weekly movement and interactive table sorting.
- **Unified History & All-Time Records**: Displays champions as `"Clay over Skippa — Kennesaw State 41, Liberty 27"` alongside the sortable All-Time Coach Leaderboard.
